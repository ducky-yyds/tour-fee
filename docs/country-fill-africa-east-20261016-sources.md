# Country coverage pack: eastern/southern Africa and South America

Research date: 2026-10-16. Reproduction: `py -X utf8 scripts/generate-country-fill-africa-east-20261016.py`.

This pack contains 21 cities in 21 previously assigned countries. Each has seven place entries, three optional experiences, five regional foods, and five named accommodation candidates. All cities use `contentTier: standard`; hotels do not count toward the ten place/experience threshold.

Prices are editorial USD planning ranges, not observed live hotel rates, ticket quotes, market extrema, or local-currency amounts. Each city separately records its local currency. Duration and approximate coordinates are planning aids. Remote natural sites require separate transfer time; they are optional and generally excluded from automatic planning.

The primary references below were searched/read for the city's culture, geography, heritage or visitor themes. They do not establish the current availability of every listed venue or the contents of every restaurant menu. Individual encyclopedia links are discovery/reference links with `checkedAt: null` unless separately verified. Hotel links identify real candidate properties or listing directories; room inventory, facilities and prices were not independently live-verified for a chosen stay date.

Additional checked place references include Windhoek City Museum (municipal site), Shorobe Basketry Cooperative (Botswana government DailyNews, article 88423), Jafuta Heritage Centre and Victoria Falls Private Game Reserve (Victoria Falls Guide), Kumbali Game Farm and Chongoni Rock Art (Malawi Tourism). The JSON stores these item-level references and limits their scope to identity/background rather than live prices.

Travel advice was checked directly on UK FCDO pages for Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, Eritrea, and Venezuela. Port Sudan, Juba and Mogadishu are marked `avoid-all-travel`; Caracas is `restricted` (advice against all but essential travel to its area). These four cities and all their place entries have automatic planning disabled. Asmara records travel permits and photography/access restrictions; this is not a claim that all of Eritrea has the same advisory level. No event date is inferred from a webpage update date.

The FCDO country pages for Ethiopia, Mozambique, Madagascar and Uganda were also checked. Ethiopia and Mozambique have serious regional exclusions; these were not incorrectly applied as a nationwide ban on Addis Ababa or Maputo. None of this establishes that travel is risk-free or that conditions cannot change.

Photos are intentionally not fabricated. City/region articles for a more specific place carry top-level `imageScope: nearby` and `imageContextNote`. Experiences use thematic/contextual imagery status. Foods with a cuisine-level, ingredient-level or insufficiently specific article have `photoStatus: needs-food-photo`; they must receive dish-specific images. Hotels have empty image URLs until a photograph of the named property is obtained, and never inherit city photos as if they depict that property.

The Guyanese Mithai description was additionally checked against the recipe author's page https://www.alicaspepperpot.com/crunchy-mithai-kurma/ to distinguish the local fried sugar-coated dough from the much broader South Asian sweets category.
